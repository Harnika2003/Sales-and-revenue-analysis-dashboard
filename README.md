Sales and Revenue Analysis Dashboard – Power BI

A professional Sales and Revenue Analysis Dashboard built in Power BI to provide actionable business insights. The dashboard visualizes sales trends, revenue contributions, and transaction patterns, enabling data-driven decisions.

Key Features:

Track overall revenue with KPI cards.

Analyze revenue by product category with column and donut charts.

Monitor monthly trends and transaction volumes using line charts.

Visualize contributions with waterfall charts.

Interactive filters and slicers for dynamic exploration.

Dataset: Retail sales data including Transaction ID, Date, Customer ID, Product Category, and Price per Unit.

Tools & Skills: Power BI, DAX, data modeling, visualization, and business analytics.

Use Case: Retail performance monitoring, sales trend analysis, and strategic decision-making.
